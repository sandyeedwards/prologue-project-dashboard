# Step 9 v1.0.15

This interface-only release refines the executive reporting hierarchy and adds optional deeper profitability analysis without increasing the default page density.

## KPI tiles

- Metric-title color now communicates the category without a decorative square beside the title.
- Values remain the dominant visual element.
- Dashboard tiles for client fees, actual cost, forecast cost, and forecast profit use a click-only question-mark control in the lower-right corner.
- Explanations stay hidden until the question mark is selected.
- Projects-in-view and logged-time summaries keep their short supporting text visible.

## Primary profitability chart

- Narrower columns and increased category spacing reduce visual bulk.
- The legend is centered and simplified.
- A dark zero baseline separates retained value from the repeated cost deduction.
- The explanatory footer is reduced to two concise statements.

## Expandable analysis

A closed-by-default **More profitability analysis** section appears directly below the primary chart on the Dashboard and in combined reports. Expanding it reveals:

1. A diverging profit/loss chart centered on zero.
2. Summary financial tiles and an operational-group breakdown table.

The expanded views use the same operational rows and calculations as the primary chart. They do not alter project P&L or count forecast cost twice.

## Health and effort

- Health panels use a smaller donut, aligned count and percentage columns, and more balanced spacing.
- Effort rows use thinner tracks and improved label/value alignment.
