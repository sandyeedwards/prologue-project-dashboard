# Step 9 v1.0.9 — Brand, health language, financial colors, and project-type facets

## Brand

The app uses a cleaned SVG interpretation of the supplied Prologue faceted-P mark. The same asset is used by Next.js as the app icon and by the header, login, and dashboard heading.

## Health language

Calculation bands are unchanged. User-facing labels are now:

- GREEN → Healthy
- AMBER → At risk
- RED → Unhealthy
- GRAY → N/A

## Project type facets

Type filtering is multi-facet rather than relying on one display string. Ready Set and DataHall projects retain their special labels and also match Scanning. Modeling remains independently filterable. `NoReport` continues to exclude a project from reporting.

## Financial colors

Client fee, target cost, actual cost, forecast cost, and forecast profit use consistent colors and outlined legend squares across portfolio, project, and comparison reporting.
