# Step 9 v1.0.35 — Historical Fill and Compare Rows

## Dashboard

Historical Revenue & Net Profit now follows only the active Dashboard project filters. The project-type selector was removed. Both cumulative series use solid-color area fills beneath their step lines.

## Compare report

The compare profitability component has two local views:

1. Entire Project — one horizontal row per selected project.
2. By Operational Group — one section per operational group and one row per selected project.

The revenue bar is the row background. Actual and remaining costs overlay it. Revenue not covered by forecast cost is forecast profit. Forecast cost beyond revenue extends in red. Profit or loss and margin remain directly visible at the right side of every row.

The Combined Project Report is not changed by this release.
